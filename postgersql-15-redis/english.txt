A PostgreSQL cluster is a single running instance managed by one Postmaster. A cluster can contain multiple databases.
PostgreSQL doesn't have separate concepts of 'users' and 'groups'-everthing is a role. A role with LOGIN privilege acts as a user, and a role without it acts as a group.
